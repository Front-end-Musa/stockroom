# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Sellers who need a straightforward workspace to manage the products they offer.

## Product Purpose

Stockroom helps sellers keep product details, current stock levels, and inventory value accurate in one place. Success means sellers can understand and maintain their catalog without losing track of availability or value.

## Positioning

A focused product-and-inventory workspace that brings catalog management, stock visibility, and stock-value awareness into the same workflow.

## Operating Context

Sellers create, update, search, paginate through, and remove product listings while reviewing product counts, total units in stock, low-stock items, and inventory value.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Angular web frontend backed by an ASP.NET Core Product API.
- Product workflows include creation, editing, deletion, server-side search, sorting, pagination, and validation.
- Product data includes name, description, price, stock, and creation date.
- API endpoints must remain environment-configured rather than hardcoded in frontend source.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Angular frontend: `full-stack_crud-frontend/`.
- Existing ASP.NET Core Product API: `first-asp.net-core-api_project/`.
- No dedicated brand assets, testimonials, pricing claims, or other marketing evidence have been supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Make inventory health understandable at a glance.
2. Keep catalog maintenance direct and low-friction.
3. Preserve accurate, current product and stock information.
4. Keep operational product work focused on the seller's immediate tasks.
